## What is nailed

Gallio at Corinth (Acts 18:12–17) plus the Delphi inscription: about AD 51–52.
Paul's eighteen months there (Acts 18:11) sit around 50–52.
1 Thessalonians (and 2 Thessalonians if taken as his) from that stay.
Romans from a later Corinth winter, collection ready (Rom 15:25–28; Acts 20:2–3): about 56–57.
Death under Nero: mid-60s if the early memory holds.

Everything else is relative.

## Method chairs

- **Letters first** (Knox and after): Gal 1–2 is sworn itinerary; Acts used where it does not fight Paul.
- **Acts plus letters**: three journeys as the skeleton; letters drop into gaps.
- **Acts as theology with travel**: cities and atmosphere; do not build year-counts on Luke alone.

Say which text you are privileging when they collide.

## Master collision: Galatians 1–2 and Acts

Paul: Arabia/Damascus, three years, short Jerusalem visit (Peter and James only), Syria-Cilicia, then fourteen years later Jerusalem with Barnabas and Titus, then Antioch clash with Peter (Gal 1:17–2:14). He swears he is not lying (1:20).

Acts has more Jerusalem visits: Acts 9, famine visit (11:27–30), council (15), greeting (18:22), collection arrest (21).

| Gal 2:1–10 identified with | Galatians date | Usual "Galatia" | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acts 15 council | mid-50s | North (ethnic) Galatia | Why no public decree? Why does Peter's collapse still fit after Acts 15? |
| Acts 11 famine visit | ~48–49, first letter | South Galatia (Acts 13–14 towns) | Why omit a later council if he wrote after it? |

Fourteen years counted from the road or from the first visit slides conversion between early and mid-30s.

## Corinth sequence (teach as a crisis, not two tidy volumes)

1. Founding stay ~50–52 (Gallio).
2. Previous letter, now lost (1 Cor 5:9).
3. Chloe + their letter (1 Cor 1:11; 7:1) → **1 Corinthians** from Ephesus ~54–55.
4. Painful visit (2 Cor 2:1; 13:2), almost always *between* the canonical letters.
5. Letter of tears (2 Cor 2:3–4; 7:8), Titus. Majority: lost. Minority: 2 Cor 10–13 *is* that letter.
6. Titus meets Paul in Macedonia → **2 Corinthians** (at least chs. 1–9) ~55–56.
7. Third visit / winter in Greece (2 Cor 12:14; 13:1; Acts 20:2–3) → **Romans**.

Whether 2 Corinthians is one letter or 1–9 plus later 10–13 is a real chair. Whether the man of 2 Cor 2 is the man of 1 Cor 5 is possible, not proven.

## Prison letters: three jails

Ephesians, Colossians, Philemon, Philippians.

- **Rome ~60–62** (Acts 28). Praetorium, Caesar's household (Phil 1:13; 4:22). Paul "old" (Phlm 9). Long walk for Onesimus.
- **Caesarea ~57–59**. Two years (Acts 23–26). Tighter than the letters' open door.
- **Ephesus ~54–56**. Not a long jail in Acts; 1 Cor 15:32 and 2 Cor 1:8–10 sound like a death-corner in Asia. Onesimus can walk home.

Theology of the four does not require settling the jail. Order relative to Romans does.

## Pastorals

1 Timothy and Titus: Paul free, men in Ephesus and Crete.
2 Timothy: late custody, finished race.
Acts ends before a death scene.

- **Released, more travel, second arrest**: 1 Tim + Titus in the gap; 2 Tim last, mid-60s. Keeps the letters inside Paul's life. Spain is Rom 15 hope plus later memory.
- **Pauline school, 80s–100**: different voice; chronology is then not Paul's calendar.

Do not jam 1 Timothy into Acts 19–20. The itinerary does not fit.

## Working teaching order (not a closed verdict)

1. Conversion, Arabia, first Jerusalem (Gal 1).
2. Cilicia / Antioch silent years.
3. First journey (Acts 13–14). *If South Galatia early: Galatians ~48–49.*
4. Council (Acts 15) — or Gal 2 already happened at the famine visit.
5. Second journey. **1–2 Thessalonians** from Corinth.
6. Third journey, Ephesus. **1 Corinthians.** Painful visit. Tear letter. Macedonia: **2 Corinthians.** *If North Galatia: Galatians here.*
7. Winter Corinth: **Romans.** Collection. Arrest.
8. Caesarea, voyage, Rome. *Prison letters here — or some earlier from Ephesus.*
9. *If release:* **1 Timothy, Titus,** then **2 Timothy.**

Hebrews stays off this list until authorship is a separate decision.

## How this lands in a session

- Date chairs go in **doctrinal / views**, not in the spoken points.
- Galatians: teach the argument either side of Acts 15; flag the visit in one sentence.
- 1–2 Corinthians: crisis sequence, not Volume I and II.
- Romans 15 is a timestamp (collection, Spain, Jerusalem fear).
- Prison four can share a file family with Rome vs Ephesus named once.
- Pastorals after the undisputed letters; second-career chair named.

Leave in the room: when Paul swears about his visits (Gal 1:20), what would it cost to let that list judge Acts — and what would it cost to let Luke's extra visits stand beside it?
